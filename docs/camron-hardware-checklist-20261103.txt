VEIL RESEARCH PROJECT — COMPUTER HARDWARE CHECKLIST
November 3, 2026 | For Camron

MINI MTC — starting screening targets, not guaranteed minimums:
- Working x86-64 mini PC or laptop; 8 GB RAM minimum, 16 GB preferred
- 4 logical CPU cores; SSD with at least 64 GB available after installing software
- Two USB ports, reliable microphone capture and simultaneous speaker output
- Stable power adapter. Battery/UPS to be separately tested.
- Avoid sleep, forced restarts or overheating during unattended recording.

SIGNAL MAIN DESKTOP — starting CPU-only local AI targets:
- 16 GB RAM minimum, 32 GB preferred
- 8 logical CPU cores target
- 100 GB free SSD, ideally 500 GB+ capacity
- Microphone/speaker, dual monitor connectivity, reliable networking
- GPU optional for existing CPU configuration; no assumption that old GPUs are compatible.

SEND THESE DETAILS BEFORE BUYING:
1. Brand and exact model
2. CPU model
3. Installed RAM
4. SSD capacity and available space
5. OS and OS license status
6. Pictures of ports and adapter (do not publicly share serial numbers)
7. Price, donation, or loan arrangement; shipping/return costs
8. Faults, missing parts and known issues

Do not purchase just because the specifications pass. Screen condition, price and fit for the intended workload. Old laptops can still have a secondary recording role.

MINI MTC ACCEPTANCE TESTS:
- Verify speaker playback and USB microphone are captured together.
- Complete 90-minute unattended trial with questions, baseline and final silence.
- Check for audio clipping, dropouts, clocks drifting, sleep events and missing files.
- Preserve unedited audio and SHA-256 hashes; test overnight only after initial success.
- Keep an independently timestamped corroborating camera/recorder where practical.
- Clean-install or inspect donated hardware before connecting to research data.
