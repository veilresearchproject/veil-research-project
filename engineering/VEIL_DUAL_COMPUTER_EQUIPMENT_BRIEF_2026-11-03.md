## TWO DISTINCT SYSTEMS

### A. Mini MTC field instrument — prioritize reliability, portability, unattended recording
- **Windows x64 mini PC:** Intel N100/N150-class or better *candidate*, 16 GB RAM preferred, internal SSD 512 GB minimum and 2 TB storage target. Confirm Windows support, USB stability, cooling and connector count against actual MTC v0.1.4 software before selection.
- **Keep microphone and playback speaker outside the case.** Reuse HyperX SoloCast and existing USB speakers first. Recorder must continuously capture both spoken prerecorded questions and ambient audio.
- **Independent corroboration** with camera and ideally secondary audio, independently powered and recorded; GoPro only after confirming compatible memory card, battery/runtime, and file rollover.
- **Case:** all-view transparent, safely ventilated, removable tray, spare room for future modules. Prior 400×300×200 mm was not approved; expansion-ready 24–30 × 18–24 × 16–24 inches is a preliminary search envelope, not final cut dimensions.
- **Electrical:** external certified AC/DC supply for initial bench runs; enclosed protected LiFePO4 system and fused appropriately engineered DC only after measured average/peak watts; 48 h is target, not a claim. EM generator remains external and OFF during response observation.
- **Gate:** preserve Oct 27 original WAV/logs, analyze read-only copies, verify audible questions, recording continuity, independent sync; then bench test 30 min → 90 min → 8 h → longer controlled endurance. Stop on failed device identity, thermal/noise, data, or power checks.

### B. SIGNAL main desktop — prioritize local AI performance, upgradeability
- **Preferred tower:** modern Ryzen 9 or Core Ultra 7/9, 128 GB RAM ideal (64 GB viable initial target), 2–4 TB NVMe SSD, expansion slots/bays, quality cooling and appropriately sized reliable PSU.
- **GPU selection by actual VRAM:** used NVIDIA RTX 3090 24 GB (cost-sensitive 24 GB path, check health/power/cooling), RTX 4090 24 GB, or RTX 5090 32 GB premium. Smaller 16 GB GPU can run smaller models but is not equivalent for model capacity. Check GPU support and driver/Ollama compatibility at delivery.
- **Plan for future interfaces:** multiple USB ports, wired networking, dual-display outputs, independent backups, microphone and voice, optional camera/depth and lab gateway. Extra instrument-compute gateways may be separate from main AI tower.
- **Existing assets:** reuse dual 24-inch monitors, keyboard, microphone and speakers. Existing Quadro P5000 desktop continues as proven CPU-mode SIGNAL prototype, not discarded before benchmark and migration.
- **Acceptance:** Windows or Linux Ollama inference on the intended GPU (not CPU fallback); timed repeatable 3B and 7B/8B conversation trials; record VRAM/RAM, latency, thermals and power; run speech and display at once; verify local-only networking and recovery after restart.
