## Fit and build gates BEFORE donation acceptance or purchase
- Verify usable **internal** length, width, height; manufacturer wall thickness, lid opening, overlap, closure strength, unsupported spans and rigidity.
- Measure real chosen Windows controller, USB connectors, SSD, mounting board, grommets, air path and cable bend radii; allow service space and room to inspect equipment.
- No reliance on pet ventilation holes as proof of electronics thermal adequacy. Measure loaded temperatures with lid shut and after 90-minute and longer runs; consider filtered quiet ventilation routed away from microphone.
- Mount PC/storage to a separate removable internal plate or stand, **not unsupported thin tank walls**. Fit guarded, strain-relieved low-voltage feedthroughs using replaceable adapter panel; minimize irreversible acrylic cuts.
- Keep mains power and protected LiFePO4 battery separate/outside at first; no battery design approval or claim of 48-hour operation until load/runtime testing.
- Confirm enclosure withstands transport vibration, handling, and service; use stable feet and protected corners; no equipment or moving fan pressed against clear wall.
- Preserve separate corroborating audio/video, visible status, session labels and photo documentation. A fully transparent cabinet is visible, but evidence quality is established by independent logging/controls, not appearance.
- Where vendor lists non-electrical intended use, ask vendor whether use as electronics housing invalidates warranty/safety assumptions.

Our 400 x 300 x 200 mm preliminary planning envelope ~= 15.75 x 11.81 x 7.87 in. A 16 x 12 in **external** enclosure cannot be assumed to accommodate a full 400 x 300 mm internal layout. Do not approve until all internal fit dimensions pass.
