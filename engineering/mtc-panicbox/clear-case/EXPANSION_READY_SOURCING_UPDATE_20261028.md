# VEIL MTC — expansion-ready, fully transparent enclosure sourcing
**Research update:** 2026-10-28. **Design only; no purchasing or construction authorized.**

## Decision
The original 400×300×200 mm / 16×12×10 in concept is **not a size limit**. The founder explicitly directed that the final transparent MTC enclosure allow future expansion. Preserve fully clear viewing sides and a transparent lid/front access, with the hardware observable on camera, rather than compromising visibility with gray/opaque walls.

Initial search **range, not a design specification**: approximately 24–30 in long × 18–24 in deep × 16–24 in high. Larger, smaller, or unconventional transparent cases (pet terraria, display cases, fish/aquarium-like acrylic boxes) remain welcome if actual usable dimensions, structural stiffness, transport, cooling, and service access are better. No holes or panels are to be fabricated from these preliminary dimensions.

## Expanded design provisions
- Windows MTC v0.1.4 unchanged. Verify actual home session_result.json, session_metadata.json, events.jsonl and audio before system integration.
- Internal removable mounting tray on freestanding aluminum extrusion/rigid standoffs (rather than loading thin acrylic side panels). Reserve approximately 25–35% of **usable mounting area** for future modules as a planning goal, subject to component fit and weight.
- Separate zones: Windows mini-PC, local ≥2 TB evidence SSD, protected low-voltage DC distribution, optional USB/data interfaces/sensor bridge, visible status panel, cable service loop. No exposed mains wiring. Prefer external certified AC-DC supply and external protected LiFePO4 power system until independent review.
- Keep room mic (initially HyperX SoloCast USB) and question playback speaker external to reduce fan contamination and confirm acoustic recording; no separate Focusrite interface required without measurement.
- Define a replaceable cable/feedthrough panel for safe incremental additions; plan guarded, quiet thermal ventilation based on measured heat load and off-axis positioning to external microphone.
- Transparent lid with repeatable closure; allow tool-free safe access to removable tray. External independent video/audio remains required; built-in status LEDs should not contaminate environmental light tests.
- Do NOT place electronics in water; no glass cutting; commercial pet-box ventilation/hinges are not certified electronics mounting or thermal designs.
- For field portability, compare the extra volume with assembled weight, mounting rig, transport damage, and acoustic isolation. Larger is not automatically better.

## Next no-purchase engineering gate
1. Measure actual mini-PC (or candidate dimensions), storage, connectors, vent/wire strain relief and removable tray.
2. Define mounting tray area **plus spare allowance**, minimum access opening and airflow path.
3. Confirm case wall thickness, lid retention/hinge design and safe supports; obtain supplier sketches/technical drawings rather than treating external size as an inside fit.
4. Run disposable, controlled Windows MTC load and heat/noise tests first; do not modify production recorder or use work computer for hardware testing.
5. Review donor offers for actual fit and safe reuse before accepting or buying; donations do not override verification gates.
