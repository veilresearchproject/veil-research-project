Verification and corrections

We preserve source-backed history, record uncertainty, and keep original private evidence outside the public site. This archive includes public posts and comments verified through Facebook Activity Log. Posted photo reviews document what VEIL examined and reported. Their image provenance and proposed explanations may require further verification. Dates are shown where established.

Research-history source documents and corrections are maintained in the VEIL GitHub archive. The archive is not a complete historical export or an automatic real-time Facebook feed.
