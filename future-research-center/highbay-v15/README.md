# VEIL Future Research Center — High-Bay Building Envelope v1.5

VEIL is an independent research initiative. The center shown here is a **PROPOSED future facility, not a constructed research building**. Geometry is an architectural visualization, not buildable drawings.

## Significant 3D building improvement
- Completed the previously open high-bay overhead envelope with 32 actual sloped standing-seam triangulated roof surface groups, folded metal edge flashings and three-dimensional seam ribs.
- Added 34 independent upper side-wall insulated rainscreen/clerestory modules (both sides of the building), 16 rear high-bay insulated/spandrel/window modules, 14 raised glass roof skylight modules and one visitor-entry cantilevered metal canopy with soffit lights.
- Total **97 new real-world-coordinate mesh groups / 876 added triangles**, grouped into 46 removable roof meshes and 51 removable wall-envelope meshes. These are actual 3D surfaces, not fake photoreal pictures. They are classed for the roof/section toggles in both renderer paths.
- All v1.4 modeled architecture, all curved MTC/ECHO/Light Cage instruments, original 13 guided tour stops, night/day lighting, navigation, cutaways and mobile menus remain.

## Verified checks
- All eight JavaScript blocks: Node syntax check passed.
- All new 97 mesh categories exported as GLB and JSON. `trimesh` GLB reload validated 97 groups, 876 triangles, finite vertices.
- Chromium with software renderer desktop 1440×850 and simulated 412×850 phone viewport: modeled 13-stop scene loads, roof cutaway toggles off/on, section view reverts, day/night changes, next stop and phone Locations drawer toggle; zero page errors.
- Actual rendered exterior screenshot comparisons differ over the building footprint. Package files pass ZIP CRC validation.

## Outstanding before photorealistic/production release
- Stylized procedural geometry is improved, but **still not photorealistic**. More high-poly structure, accurate room-scale interiors, environment lighting/UV-PBR textures/photographic material detail and professional 3D art refinement are needed.
- GPU/WebGL renderer shader compilation and frame rate remain UNVERIFIED on GPU hardware; headless Chromium did not expose WebGL. Physical Samsung Galaxy S25 performance, touch ergonomics, code/accessibility and architectural engineering not validated.
- Do not confuse visual concept images in `assets` with rendered 3D views; the actual model screenshots are in the archive.
- Do not merge GitHub development drafts, publish on the live VEIL website or post to Facebook until explicit owner approval.
