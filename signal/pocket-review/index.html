What this is — and isn't

This is a deliberately limited offline phone viewer with optional in-memory human triage, not a live connection to SIGNAL on your PC. It cannot play source WAV files, identify voices, command VEIL instruments, or certify evidence. A human must review candidate audio against ordinary explanations and controls.
