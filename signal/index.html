OUR PROMISE TO VISITORS

What SIGNAL is—and isn't

He is: a scripted, evolving digital host for VEIL who makes science more approachable, welcomes skeptical questions, and openly admits uncertainty.

He isn't: a conscious being, a live laboratory monitoring system, or an AI automatically learning from your activity. His responses and development milestones are reviewed and authored.

The future: we aim to connect a separate, controlled AI reasoning system to verified research records. When that happens, we'll label AI-generated explanations and cite the underlying evidence.

CHAPTER 04 / FIRST REAL DATA UTILITY · DETERMINISTIC, LOCAL

SIGNAL's Sensor Data Audit Bench

This tool performs actual calculations on CSV data you choose. It checks timestamp order, missed sampling intervals, and step changes in temperature for each sensor separately. It does not require a cloud AI service, connect to instruments, determine causes, or prove paranormal activity.

CSV headers: timestamp,temperature_c plus optional sensor_id. Timestamps must include timezone (e.g. 2026-10-22T08:00:00Z). Data remain in this browser page.
Flags gaps greater than 1.5 × this interval, within each sensor stream.

Evidence integrity: SHA-256 fingerprints are computed when the browser permits, but a hash does not establish authenticity or chain of custody. This is an experimental quality-control utility, not a validated scientific instrument. All flags require human review and independent controls. The example data are invented for demonstration.
