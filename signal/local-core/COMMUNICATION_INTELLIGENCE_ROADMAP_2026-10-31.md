# SIGNAL Communication Intelligence Roadmap — 2026-10-31

Captured from Camron's feedback and the subsequent VEIL discussion. Carry forward into the next SIGNAL desktop development session.

## Development priorities
1. **English first.** Get reliable one-on-one local English listening, conversation, natural responses, speaking, interruptions, and error recovery before multilingual expansion.
2. **Microphone and recognition benchmarking.** Test existing microphones and room noise before purchasing additional equipment. Evaluate offline/local speech recognition options for accuracy, latency and resource demands.
3. **Optional pronunciation calibration.** Let users choose to contribute pronunciation examples or correct recurring misrecognitions of names, accents and technical terminology. Never assume every speaker needs calibration. Consent-based, removable and stored locally.
4. **Adaptive explanation level.** Start with clear language; allow explicit “simpler / more technical” preferences and corrections. Do not infer intelligence, literacy or education from speech. Keep user-specific preferences optional, editable and private; avoid unconsented background profiling.
5. **International readiness.** Avoid English-only hardcoding in data structures; allow language/locale selection later for recognition, conversation and TTS. Full multilingual voice is a later milestone after English performance is verified.
6. **Global collaboration.** Investigate reputable international anomalous-phenomena and instrument-research groups for VEIL collaboration; verify organizations and contacts before outreach. No partnerships assumed.

## Acceptance criteria for upcoming desktop milestone
- Demonstrate end-to-end local English conversation using existing hardware.
- Measure recognition errors across at least several spoken sentences including VEIL/MTC terminology; record manual corrections without capturing audio by default.
- Show appropriate plain-language and technical explanations on demand.
- Preserve evidence-first safeguards: no fabricated sensor access, no false EVP certainty, no automatic Panic Box ALL CLEAR.
- Keep voice/microphone opt-in and local-first. No paid services without approval.

This is a documented roadmap, not a claim that these capabilities have been implemented.
