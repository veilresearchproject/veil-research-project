Temperature Curtain

Can synchronized temperature measurements map movement?

A proposed distributed thermal measurement grid. Interpretation of moving patterns or volumetric shapes requires validation and independent physical references.

Andrew Underwood · Founder & Research DirectorErie region · Pennsylvania
Research framework

Virtual model

The research concept includes a 196-sensor virtual configuration and an initial 1.5°C event threshold, subject to calibration.

Tests

Use controlled heat and cold sources, track trailing patterns and assess effects from HVAC, people, equipment and room geometry.

Validation

Correlate sensor maps with reference thermal imaging and conventional temperature sources.

This initiative is in concept and protocol development. Experimental claims have not been physically validated.
Four-gate scientific readiness · October 10, 2026

Calibrate before interpreting spatial patterns

These are requirements and decision criteria to develop—not completed validation or an indication that all four gates have been passed.

1 · Prior work

Thermal arrays and distributed temperature monitoring predate VEIL. A specific sensor-network prior-art review remains to be completed.

2 · Physical basis

Sensors measure temperature at their own positions. Reconstructing three-dimensional body-shaped volumes would require validated spatial models and independent references.

3 · Measurable outcome

Compare a small array with calibrated known hot/cold sources and reference imaging. Prespecify time lag, location error, drift and false-alarm performance.

4 · Stop or revise

Do not scale up unless controlled disturbances can be localized and airflow, equipment heating and sensor faults can be distinguished.
