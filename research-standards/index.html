Core Principles & Research Standards

“We respect your experience. We investigate your evidence.”

At VEIL Research Project, extraordinary claims deserve careful investigation—not automatic acceptance or dismissal. Our mission is not to prove paranormal phenomena exist, nor to prove they do not. Our mission is to follow the evidence wherever it leads.

Our 11 Core Principles

  1. 01

    Evidence Before Belief

    Facts and evidence take precedence over personal beliefs and expectations.

  2. 02

    Respect People. Question Claims.

    We respect those who share their experiences, but every claim remains open to examination.

  3. 03

    Verify Before Amplifying

    Photographs, recordings, videos, and stories should be checked for authenticity and origin whenever possible.

  4. 04

    Ordinary Explanations Come First

    We examine conventional explanations before considering extraordinary possibilities.

  5. 05

    Unexplained Does Not Mean Paranormal

    An unknown cause is not automatically a supernatural one.

  6. 06

    No Manufactured Agreement

    We will not compromise our findings simply to gain approval, followers, or popularity.

  7. 07

    Transparency and Accountability

    We explain conclusions, acknowledge uncertainty, and correct mistakes when identified.

  8. 08

    Protect the Integrity of Evidence

    Original evidence, sources, timestamps, and relevant documentation matter.

  9. 09

    Technology Assists. Humans Decide.

    Technology supports investigations, but human judgment and verification remain essential.

  10. 10

    Independence, Ethics, and Scientific Integrity

    We welcome constructive criticism, protect privacy, and reject predetermined conclusions.

  11. 11

    Full Disclosure — No Selective Reporting

    VEIL will report every completed investigation’s outcome, whether potentially anomalous, explained, inconclusive, or a failed experiment. We will not suppress unfavorable findings, exaggerate results, or select cases for publication to attract viewers. We will document methods, limitations, and corrections, maintain an auditable case register, and protect confidential identities or sensitive material through consent, redaction, or restricted access. Public summaries will account for any findings that cannot be released in full.

Our Commitment

We understand that some findings may challenge popular beliefs or personal interpretations. Our intention is never to embarrass, ridicule, or dismiss anyone.

But we will not sacrifice objectivity simply to make a claim more appealing.

Respect the person. Investigate the evidence. Report the findings.

That is the standard we expect from ourselves, and the standard by which we welcome others to evaluate our work.
