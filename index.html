AUFOUNDER'S MARK

Meet Andrew Underwood

Founder & Lead Researcher · VEIL Research Project

Andrew Byron Underwood is the founder and lead researcher of VEIL Research Project, an emerging independent initiative based in the Erie, Pennsylvania region. Drawing on his past experience investigating reported paranormal activity and his professional background in industrial manufacturing, estimating, and technical sourcing, Andrew is working to bring disciplined engineering questions to a field often dominated by personal interpretation.

His goal is to develop interoperable experimental systems that preserve raw evidence, synchronize measurements, establish reliable baselines, and actively test ordinary explanations before drawing conclusions. VEIL's current work includes proposed optical, thermal, electromagnetic, and observer-free audio experiments, alongside portable monitoring and controlled-chamber concepts.

Andrew envisions a dedicated research center where engineers, scientists, skeptical reviewers, and field investigators can collaborate on repeatable testing. The center and its instruments remain proposals in development, not a funded or physically validated laboratory.

Andrew originated the project's vision and experimental directions, with AI assistance in refining proposals, documentation, and virtual test plans. He welcomes rigorous questions and independent review.

Research framework

The question

What if we stopped asking whether to believe—and started asking what we can measure?

Why VEIL

Establish baseline conditions, preserve raw data and deliberately challenge unusual results with conventional explanations. The aim is repeatable research—not a claim that paranormal phenomena have been proven.

Engineering milestone · October 23, 2026

MTC completes 11-question unattended bench trial

VEIL's Windows-based Mobile Trans-Communication (MTC) prototype played 11 prerecorded questions through desktop speakers and recorded the room continuously using a HyperX SoloCast USB microphone. A 5-minute, 27-second unattended session completed, and the saved acoustic recording was manually reviewed.

Each question was followed by a 15-second response interval. The software's integrity review returned PASS_WITH_MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED; the researcher then confirmed audibly that the prerecorded questions were captured in the saved microphone WAV file. This is an initial engineering demonstration, not independent validation or evidence of paranormal communication.

Core AI development requirement · VEIL-AI-001

A laboratory where researchers can talk to their instruments

VEIL is designing a voice-first, conversational AI interface for its proposed research center so scientists, engineers and investigators can work hands-free, speak observations and questions naturally, and receive spoken responses without constant manual data entry.

Speak, ask, document

Authorized researchers would verbally request readings, initiate supported workflows and record observations as attributed, timestamped experiment notes linked to synchronized sensor data.

Connected research systems

The proposed interface would connect research instruments approved for development through shared experiment context and data retrieval. Exploratory concepts would be integrated only if future scientific review justifies their testing.

Scientific integrity and safety

Critical equipment actions require authorization, confirmation and independent safety interlocks. Silent and observer-free recording modes protect sensitive experiments, including MTC/EVP testing, from voice contamination.

Development status: mandatory architectural requirement and proposed capability. A laboratory-wide voice AI interface has not yet been built, deployed or validated.

Research portfolio · scientific readiness review · October 10, 2026

Methods before claims.

VEIL distinguishes preliminary research protocols, supporting instruments and exploratory hypotheses. These classifications are planning decisions, not scientific results.

Current status: MTC has completed initial physical playback-and-recording bench tests. No controlled paranormal experiments, independently validated instrument performance or evidence of paranormal phenomena are claimed. Full literature and patent reviews, independent scientific review, and calibrated trials remain outstanding.

Four gates before scientific trials

1 · Previous work

Find existing experiments, technology and null findings. Document what has and has not been attempted.

2 · Physical basis

Separate established measurement physics from unverified assumptions and speculative mechanisms.

3 · Measurable outcomes

Prespecify the endpoints, controls, calibrations, blinding and analysis required for a meaningful test.

4 · Stop or revise

State in advance what results would refute the idea, trigger redesign or halt further investment.
